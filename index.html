HOW TO PLAY

1. CONTROLS

Press SPACE, click or tap to jump.

2. AVOID PIPES

Fly between the green pipes without touching them.

3. EARN POINTS

Every pair of pipes you pass gives you 1 point.

4. COLLECT COINS

Earn coins by passing pipes. Harder levels give more coins!

5. BUY SKINS

Use your coins in the Skin Shop to unlock new characters.

6. DIFFICULTY

EASY: Slow pipes and large gaps.
MEDIUM: Normal speed and gaps.
HARD: Fast pipes and small gaps.

7. GAME OVER

Hitting a pipe, the ground or the ceiling ends the game.

PAUSED

Tap anywhere on the game to jump · P to pause
